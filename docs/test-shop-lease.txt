SHOP LEASE AGREEMENT
Made-up demo document. All names, dates, and amounts are fictional.

1. PARTIES
This lease is between Chanda Properties Limited (the Landlord) and Mwila Tailoring (the Tenant).

2. PREMISES
The Landlord lets Shop 14, Kabulonga Arcade, Lusaka, to the Tenant. The shop measures 32 square metres and includes one storage room. The Tenant may use the shop only for tailoring, clothing sales, and alterations.

3. LEASE PERIOD
The lease starts on 1 February 2027 and ends on 31 January 2029. This is a term of 24 months.

4. RENT
Monthly rent is ZMW 6,500. Rent is payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. Rent increases by 8% on 1 February 2028.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant must pay a security deposit of ZMW 13,000 before receiving the keys. The Landlord will return the deposit within 30 days after the lease ends, less the cost of any damage or unpaid rent.

6. LATE PAYMENT
If rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, the Tenant must pay a late fee of ZMW 400. A further ZMW 50 applies for each extra day the rent remains unpaid, up to a maximum of ZMW 1,500 in any one month.

7. UTILITIES
The Tenant pays for electricity through a prepaid meter. Water and refuse collection are included in the rent. The Tenant pays for its own internet connection.

8. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE
The Landlord repairs the roof, outside walls, and main water pipes. The Tenant keeps the inside of the shop clean and repairs any damage it causes, including broken windows and door locks. Urgent faults must be reported within 48 hours.

9. ALTERATIONS AND SIGNAGE
The Tenant must get written consent from the Landlord before making any alterations. The Tenant may put up one sign above the shop door, no wider than 2 metres.

10. INSURANCE
The Landlord insures the building. The Tenant must insure its own stock, sewing machines, and equipment.

11. SUBLETTING
The Tenant may not sublet or share the shop without written consent from the Landlord.

12. ENDING THE LEASE EARLY
Either party may end this lease early by giving 3 months' written notice. If the Tenant leaves without giving notice, the Tenant must pay 2 months' rent as compensation.

13. RENEWAL
The Tenant may ask to renew the lease for a further 24 months by giving written notice at least 90 days before the lease ends.

14. DISPUTES
The parties will first try to settle any dispute through mediation in Lusaka. If mediation fails within 30 days, either party may go to court.

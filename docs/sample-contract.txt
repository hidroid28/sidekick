SAMPLE TAILORING SERVICE AGREEMENT (made-up demo data)

This agreement is between Mwila Tailoring and Sunrise Primary School.

Work: Mwila Tailoring will make 120 school uniforms.

Payment: The school will pay a 40% deposit before work starts. The balance is due within 14 days of delivery.

Delivery: All uniforms must be delivered by 15 January 2027.

Late delivery: The price falls by 2% for each full week of late delivery, up to a maximum of 10%.

Cancellation: The school may cancel with 7 days written notice. The deposit is not refunded once cloth has been bought.
